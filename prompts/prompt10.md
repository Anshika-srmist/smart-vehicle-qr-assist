# Smart Vehicle QR Assist - Premium Visual Redesign

Redesign the entire application UI/UX.

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT make this look like a car rental website.

Do NOT make this look like an automobile showroom.

Do NOT make this look like an e-commerce vehicle marketplace.

The vehicles are visual storytelling elements only.

The actual product is:

"Anonymous communication between the public and vehicle owners using QR technology."
